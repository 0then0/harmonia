# Code of conduct

Harmonia welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help keep issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence; give specific, constructive feedback.
- Welcome questions and respect different experiences, identities and viewpoints.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for the effects of your words and actions.
- Respect privacy. Share another person's personal information only with their permission.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, doxxing and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. These expectations also apply when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the [project maintainer, 0then0](https://github.com/0then0). To request a private contact channel, open an [issue](https://github.com/0then0/Harmonia/issues/new) titled “Private conduct contact requested” without incident details or personal information. Share sensitive evidence only through a channel agreed with the maintainer. Do not use vulnerability reports for conduct complaints.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close or lock a discussion, issue a warning or restrict participation, depending on severity and repeated behavior. Reports and personal information should be shared only as needed to address the concern. Moderation decisions should explain the applicable behavior and restriction without exposing private details.

These expectations apply to maintainers as well as other participants. Reports involving the maintainer cannot receive independent review through this project; GitHub's reporting and blocking tools remain available. Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith is unacceptable.
